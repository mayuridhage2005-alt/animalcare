Rescue Awareness Camp

📅 Date: July 2026

⏰ Time: 10:00 AM - 3:00 PM

📍 Location: Tulsi Bhag , Pune

The Rescue Awareness Camp is an initiative designed to educate and empower individuals to take active roles in animal welfare. This camp focuses on spreading awareness about how to identify animals in distress, provide immediate assistance, and safely contact rescue services. Participants will learn basic first-aid techniques for injured animals, understand the importance of timely intervention, and gain insights into responsible community behavior toward animals. The program also highlights how small actions by individuals can create a big impact in protecting vulnerable animals. Through live demonstrations, interactive sessions, and guidance from experienced volunteers and professionals, attendees will develop confidence in handling real-life situations involving animal rescue. This camp aims to build a compassionate community where people are aware, responsible, and ready to help animals in need.
