Adoption Drive

📅 Date: August 2026

⏰ Time: 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM

📍 Location: Powai Garden, Mumbai

The Adoption Drive is a meaningful initiative aimed at giving rescued and abandoned animals a second chance at life. This event connects animals in need with compassionate individuals and families who are ready to provide them with a safe, loving, and permanent home. Visitors will have the opportunity to meet and interact with a variety of rescued animals, understand their personalities, and receive guidance on choosing the right pet according to their lifestyle. Our team ensures that every adoption is carried out responsibly, with proper counseling and awareness about long-term commitment and animal care. The event also focuses on spreading awareness about the importance of adopting pets instead of buying them, thereby reducing the number of homeless animals. Experts and volunteers will be present to answer questions, provide care tips, and assist throughout the adoption process. By participating in this drive, you are not just adopting a pet—you are saving a life and becoming part of a compassionate movement that values kindness, responsibility, and respect for all living beings.
