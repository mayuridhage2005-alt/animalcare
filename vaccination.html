Vaccination Camp

📅 Date: June 2026

⏰ Time: 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM

📍 Location: Sanjay Gandhi National Park, Mumbai

The Vaccination Camp is a crucial initiative aimed at safeguarding the health and well-being of both stray and domestic animals. This event focuses on providing free vaccinations, basic medical checkups, and preventive care to animals that often do not have access to regular veterinary services. Our experienced team of veterinarians and volunteers work tirelessly to ensure that each animal receives proper attention, care, and treatment. The camp also plays an important role in controlling the spread of diseases such as rabies and infections, making the environment safer for both animals and humans. In addition to medical services, the camp promotes awareness among pet owners and the general public about responsible animal care, hygiene, and the importance of regular health checkups. Visitors can interact with professionals, ask questions, and learn how to better care for their pets. By participating in this camp, you are contributing to a healthier ecosystem where animals are protected, cared for, and respected.
